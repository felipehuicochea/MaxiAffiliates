# MaxiAffiliates affiliate guide

(Español: [AFFILIATE_GUIDE.es.md](AFFILIATE_GUIDE.es.md))

## Join
1. Open the signup page the store gave you.
2. Enter your email and choose how you want to be paid:
   - **Lightning:** a Lightning address such as `you@walletofsatoshi.com`, or an LNURL.
   - **On-chain:** a Bitcoin address.
3. Some stores approve affiliates by hand. Your link starts working once you're *Active*.

## Share your link
Your link looks like `https://…/aff/STORE/CODE`. When someone clicks it and buys within 30 days, the sale is yours. The first affiliate link they clicked wins. Buying through your own link doesn't count.

## Your panel
- **Signing in:** go to the panel address the store gave you (`…/plugins/maxiaffiliates/STORE/panel/login`) and enter your email. You'll get a sign-in link that works once and expires in 15 minutes. If the store can't send email, it will give you a personal panel link instead. Keep that link private.
- **What you'll see:**
  - your link and code;
  - link clicks, referred customers, sales, and total earned and paid out;
  - your balance:
    - *pending:* still in the store's hold period, which leaves time for refunds;
    - *payable*;
    - *refund clawbacks*;
    - the amount of your next payout;
  - every commission and your payout history.
- **Payout address:** you can change it in the panel. It applies to future payouts. Saving signs you out on your other devices, and you'll get an email about the change.
- **Sign out** when you're done on a shared computer.

## Getting paid
Payouts go out weekly or monthly in bitcoin once your balance reaches the store's minimum. Smaller balances carry over. If a customer is refunded after you were paid, that amount is deducted from your next payout.

## Safety
The store will never ask for your password or seed phrase; the panel has no password at all. Only use sign-in links you asked for. If you get an "address changed" email you didn't expect, contact the store immediately.
